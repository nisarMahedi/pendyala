Location & Directions

Pendyala ek Mehdhavi gaon hai. Hamara gaon asal mein 3 gaon milkar bana hai. Ye teen gaon hain:

Pendyala Colony

(Mehdhiyabad)

Pendyala Colony hamare gaon ka ek hissa hai aur ye Pendyala ka entrance bhi hai.

Yahan taqreeban tamam log Mehdhavi Musalman hain.

Yahan 2 Masjids maujood hain.

Pendyala

Ye hamare gaon ka markazi aur sab se bada hissa hai.

Taqreeban:

  • 97% Muslims
  • 2% Hindus
  • 1% Christians

(Ye sirf taqreebi andaza hai.)

Kotha Pendyala

(Kothuru)

Kotha Pendyala hamare gaon ke bilkul qareeb hai.

Yahan ke zyada tar log Pendyala ke khandanon se talluq rakhte hain.

Dono gaon ke darmiyan sirf 300-400 meter ka faasla hai.

Yahan taqreeban tamam abaadi Hindu hai.

In teenon gaon ki tareekh aur ye kis tarah bane, uske baare mein jaanne ke liye History page zaroor dekhein.

🚙 By Road

Alhamdulillah, aaj ke daur mein Pendyala pahunchna pehle ke muqabale kaafi aasaan ho gaya hai. Ab sadkein aur transport dono behtar hain.

National Highway 65 hamare gaon se sirf 2 km door hai. Pendyala Colony National Highway se taqreeban 1.2 km door hai.

Highway par hamare sab se qareeb gaon ka naam Keesara hai. Agar aap Keesara pahunch jaate hain aur kisi se Pendyala ka rasta poochte hain, to In Sha Allah log aapki madad zaroor karenge.

Agar aap Hyderabad ki taraf se aa rahe hain, to Vijayawada ki bus pakad kar Keesara mein utar jaiye.

Agar aap Vijayawada ki taraf se aa rahe hain, to Hyderabad wali bus pakad kar pehle Toll Plaza yani Keesara mein utar jaiye.

Keesara se Pendyala ke liye share autos subah 6:00 AM se raat 10:00 PM tak aasani se mil jaate hain.

Auto ka kiraya sirf ₹10 per person hai.

Agar aap North India ya kisi aur state se aa rahe hain, to pehle Hyderabad ya Vijayawada pahunchiye, phir wahan se Vijayawada–Hyderabad route ki bus lekar Keesara utar jaiye.

🚆 By Train

Hamare sab se qareeb railway station Vijayawada Junction hai.

Ye Bharat ke sab se bade aur busy railway stations mein se ek hai.

Pendyala yahan se taqreeban 60 km door hai.

Railway Station se bahar aakar Hyderabad ya Nandigama ki bus pakad lijiye aur Keesara mein utar jaiye. Wahan se auto lekar aasani se Pendyala pahunch sakte hain.

✈️ By Air

Hamare qareeb do airports hain.

Rajiv Gandhi International Airport (Hyderabad)

Agar aap India ke bahar se aa rahe hain, to ye sab se behtar airport hai.

Airport se Vijayawada ki bus ya taxi lekar Keesara pahunchiye, phir auto se Pendyala aa sakte hain.

Vijayawada International Airport

Airport se Vijayawada Bus Stand pahunchiye.

Wahan se Hyderabad ki bus lekar Keesara mein utar jaiye.

Phir auto se Pendyala aa sakte hain.

Welcome to Pendyala

Jab aap National Highway se Pendyala ki taraf aate hain, to sab se pehle Pendyala Colony nazar aati hai.

Raaste mein aapko hariyali, phalon ki dukanein, mehnat karne wale log aur gaon ka sukoon bhara mahaul dekhne ko milega.

Har gaon ki apni ek pehchaan hoti hai, aur Pendyala ki pehchaan uske log, unka apnapan aur unki mehnat hai.

Jo log Pendyala mein paida hue hain aur padhai, naukri ya dusre kaamon ki wajah se gaon se door rehte hain, unke liye wapas Pendyala aana ek khaas ehsaas hota hai.

Hamari dua hai ke jo bhi Pendyala aaye, uska safar aasaan ho aur yahan ki yaadein hamesha uske saath rahen.

Agar aap pehli baar Pendyala aa rahe hain aur raste ke baare mein koi madad chahiye, to gaon ke log In Sha Allah aapki madad zaroor karenge.
