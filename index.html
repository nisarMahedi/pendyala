Assalamualaikum!

Aap sab ka Pendyala ki website par dil se khair maqdam hai.

Pendyala sirf ek gaon nahi, balki hamari pehchaan, hamari tehzeeb aur hamari yaadon ka hissa hai. Is website ko banane ka maqsad ye hai ke hamare gaon ki tareekh, riwayat, aham jagahen aur zaroori maloomat ek hi jagah par mehfooz rahein.

Pehle mera iradah tha ke Pendyala ke baare mein ek kitaab likhun. Lekin aaj kal zyada tar log internet ka istemal karte hain. Is liye maine socha ke hamare gaon ki bhi apni ek website honi chahiye, taake aane wali naslein bhi apne gaon ki tareekh aur pehchaan ko jaan saken.

Agar aap Pendyala se talluq rakhte hain, to umeed hai ke ye website aapko apne gaon ki yaadein taza karegi. Aur agar aap pehli baar Pendyala ke baare mein jaan rahe hain, to humein umeed hai ke ye website aapko hamare gaon ko behtar tareeqe se jaanne ka mauqa degi.

Pendyala ek Mehdhavi gaon hai. Agar aap bhi Mehdhavi hain, to humein umeed hai ke Buzurgan-e-Deen ke sadqe se aapko is gaon se ek khaas apnapan aur roohani sukoon mehsoos hoga.

Khush Aamdeed!
